A clearer path to local pinball repair.

Tip Top Pinball repairs pinball and arcade machines around Richmond. I redesigned and launched its website, bringing the repair services, the areas it covers, service requests and the machines it has on location into one place.

Before and after

From a single Google Sites page to a website built around the service.

The work

I carried the project from the original page through design, launch and ongoing support.

Design and development

Make the service clear.

The homepage leads with the repair offer, lists the faults the shop fixes, and gives customers one direct way to request service.

Content and local search

Connect the business to its area.

I wrote service-area pages for Henrico, Chesterfield, Ashland, Glen Allen and Midlothian, and set up the Google Business Profile so local searches lead to the site.

Machines on location

Help players find a place to play.

The on-location page groups each venue’s address and machine lineup, with machine names, makers and years. Every venue links to its own Pinball Map listing, which the page credits as the source.

See the on-location page ↗(opens in a new tab)

Launch and upkeep

Look after it once it’s live.

I launched the site on the business’s own domain and maintain it monthly. It runs as static HTML, CSS and JavaScript on GitHub Pages, with Web3Forms handling service requests.
